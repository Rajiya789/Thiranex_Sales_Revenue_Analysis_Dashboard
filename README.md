# Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

## Thiranex Internship Project

A data analysis and visualization project focused on analyzing sales and revenue performance, tracking business KPIs, identifying top-performing products and customers, analyzing monthly sales trends, and generating business insights.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales and revenue data and provide meaningful insights into business performance using Python and data visualization techniques.

The project focuses on:

* Tracking important business KPIs
* Analyzing sales by category
* Analyzing monthly sales trends
* Identifying top-performing products
* Identifying top customers
* Analyzing profit by customer segment
* Creating interactive filters
* Generating business insights

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Plotly
* IPyWidgets
* Google Colab

## Data Analysis

### Sales by Category

The project analyzes sales across different categories:

* Technology
* Office Supplies
* Other
* Furniture

Technology is the largest sales category with approximately **1.30M** in sales.

### Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly sales are analyzed from January to July.

The highest displayed monthly sales value is:

* **July: 0.26M**
* **May: 0.23M**

### Top 5 Products

The project identifies the top five products based on sales.

The highest-selling displayed product is:

**Power Bank Shell 5V - 0.34M**

### Top 5 Customers

The project identifies the top five customers based on sales.

The highest-selling displayed customer is:

**Xylona Preis - 0.27M**

### Profit by Segment

Profit distribution is analyzed across:

* Corporate
* Home Office
* Consumer

The Consumer segment represents approximately **67.44%** of the displayed profit percentage.
